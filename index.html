MSME Sustainable ZED Certification

ZED promotes Zero Defect and Zero Effect practices to help MSMEs improve quality, productivity and sustainability.

Bronze

Entry level certification focused on basic systems and operational practices.

Silver

Focused on process improvement, standardization and stronger management systems.

Gold

Advanced level focused on manufacturing excellence, innovation and sustainability.

ZED Eligibility

Who Can Participate?

Manufacturing MSMEs registered on the Udyam Registration portal are eligible to participate.
